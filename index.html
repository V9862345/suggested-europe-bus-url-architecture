How a URL is built

Every URL follows one simple path, from the general to the specific.

/destinations/Section
france/Country
paris/City
prices/Page group
weddings/Page

URL rules

  • Use lowercase letters only, with hyphens between words (private-coach-hire).
  • Do not end URLs with .html. End them with a slash (/services/).
  • Use plain English words only. No codes or language prefixes.
  • Group pages into folders: Section › Country › City › Page.
  • Redirect every old URL to its new URL with a 301 redirect, so no page is lost.
